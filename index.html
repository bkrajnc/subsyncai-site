Why SubSyncAI?

Subtitles that speak your language.

A simple desktop helper and a browser extension let you translate subtitles on the fly — with no complicated setup.

🌍

Up to 50 languages locally

The built-in local translator is included in your subscription. For basic translation you don’t need an API key or to pay for tokens.

✨

Choose your AI

For more advanced translations you can connect supported AI providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or your own endpoint. API usage costs are paid to the provider.

⚡

Translates while you watch

SubSyncAI works in your desktop browser together with the Chrome or Edge extension and shows translated subtitles during playback.

🔐

Local mode

When you use the local translator, no text has to be sent to an external AI API for translation. Ideal for users who want simplicity and control.

📺

From your computer to the TV

Send the picture to your TV via HDMI, a USB‑C/DisplayPort video output, wireless display (if both devices support it) or another screen-mirroring method.

💬

Fewer language barriers

A film, series or documentary becomes more accessible to family, guests and users for whom the officially offered subtitle languages don’t work.
