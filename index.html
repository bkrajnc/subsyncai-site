FAQ

Good to know

Why do I need my own AI key?

Running translation through our own servers at scale would mean charging far more per month to cover AI costs for every customer. Instead, you connect your own account with the AI provider you prefer, pay them directly at their normal rate, and your subscription to SubSync AI stays a flat, low fee for the app itself.

How much does the AI translation itself cost?

It depends on the provider and model you choose, but budget models like Claude Haiku typically cost a few cents per hour of subtitles. You can see live reference pricing for every model right inside the app's settings before you choose one.

Which streaming platforms are supported?

SubSync AI currently supports Netflix in Chrome and Edge. Support for additional platforms is being added over time.

Does this affect my Netflix account or violate its terms?

No. SubSync AI only reads the subtitle text that Netflix already displays on your own screen and shows a translated overlay on top of it in your browser. It doesn't modify Netflix, access your account credentials, or touch any video stream.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Each license activates on one device at a time. If you switch computers, you can deactivate the old device from the app's License window and activate the new one instantly.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes - cancel from your Lemon Squeezy customer portal link (sent in your purchase email) whenever you like. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
