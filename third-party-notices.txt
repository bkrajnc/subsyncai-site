SubSync AI does not ship these components inside its installer. The setup program
installs them on your computer from their official sources (python.org for Python, the
Python Package Index PyPI for the packages), into a private folder of the app
(backend\venv). The license text of every package is installed together with it, in
backend\venv\Lib\site-packages\<package>-<version>.dist-info\ (LICENSE / licenses
folder). Versions below are the ones tested in October 2026; newer compatible versions
may be installed.
